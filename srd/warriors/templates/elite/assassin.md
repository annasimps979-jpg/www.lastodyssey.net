Assassins are fast, deadly attackers that can quickly take out more fragile [party](/srd/glossary#term-party) members before they have a chance to act.

### Available Abilities

|Name|[Type](/srd/combat/attacks#term-ability-type)|Cost|Description|
|:-|:-|:-|:-|
|Mark Target|[Ranged](/srd/combat/attacks#term-ranged)|1 [AP](/srd/combat/round#term-ap)|The Assassin inflicts the Hunted [status](/srd/combat/statuses) on the target.  When they use a [spell](/srd/combat/attacks#term-spell), [strike](/srd/combat/attacks#term-strike), or [sleight](/srd/combat/attacks#term-sleight) on the target, their player casts the [hit die](/srd/combat/attacks#term-hit-die) twice and chooses the number they prefer.  This does not apply to attacks that target multiple [warriors](/srd/warriors).  Only one warrior can have the Hunted status inflicted on them per Assassin in the [party](/srd/glossary#term-party).  If a Hunted warrior dies due to one of the Assassin's attacks, they may immediately use Mark Target on another warrior at no AP cost.|
|Strike|[Melee](/srd/combat/attacks#term-melee)|3 [MP](/srd/heroes/numbers#term-mp), 1 [AP](/srd/combat/round#term-ap)|The Assassin makes a [colorless](/srd/combat/elements#term-colorless) strike that deals 1.5x damage on a hit in addition to other modifiers.|
|Charm (Physical Attack)|[Ally](/srd/combat/attacks#term-ally-ability)|3 [MP](/srd/heroes/numbers#term-mp), 1 [AP](/srd/combat/round#term-ap)|The Assassin moves Physical Attack a single step up the [Bonus Tracker](/srd/combat/glamors#term-bonus-tracker) for the selected target.|
|Hex (Physical Defense)|[Ranged](/srd/combat/attacks#term-ranged)|2 [MP](/srd/heroes/numbers#term-mp), 1 [AP](/srd/combat/round#term-ap)|The Assassin moves a single target down one level on the [Bonus Tracker](/srd/combat/glamors#term-bonus-tracker) for their Physical Defense.|
