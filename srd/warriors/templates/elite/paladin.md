### Available Abilities

|Name|[Type](/srd/combat/attacks#term-ability-type)|Cost|Description|
|:-|:-|:-|:-|
|Focus|[Self](/srd/combat/attacks#term-self-ability)|1 [AP](/srd/combat/round#term-ap)|The Paladin applies the Focused [status](/srd/combat/statuses) to themselves.  While they are Focused, they may only use their [basic attack](/srd/combat/round#term-basic-attack).  The Paladin also gain a flat +2 bonus to the damage they deal when they hit a warrior with a [strike](/srd/combat/attacks#term-strike) or [spell](/srd/combat/attacks#term-spell).  Every time they hit a warrior, this bonus increases by 1.  When they miss, the Focused status goes away.|
|Strike|[Melee](/srd/combat/attacks#term-melee)|3 [MP](/srd/heroes/numbers#term-mp), 1 [AP](/srd/combat/round#term-ap)|The Paladin makes a [colorless](/srd/combat/elements#term-colorless) strike that deals 1.5x damage on a hit in addition to other modifiers.|
|Taunt|[Ranged](/srd/combat/attacks#term-ranged)|1 [AP](/srd/combat/round#term-ap)|The Paladin uses a [sleight](/srd/combat/attacks#term-sleight) that inflicts the Taunted [status](/srd/combat/statuses) on a single target on a hit until the beginning of the Paladin's next turn, not counting held turns.  A Taunted [character](/srd/glossary#term-character) can only use single-target [spells](/srd/combat/attacks#term-spell), [strikes](/srd/combat/attacks#term-strike), and sleight [abilities](/srd/glossary#term-ability), and only on the character who inflicted it.  It can also use the [Move](/srd/combat/round#term-move) [action](/srd/glossary#term-action) to move into range of their target if needed.  Each character can only be Taunted by a single other character at a time.  If a Taunted character cannot affect the Paladin by any means, they will act as though they are not Taunted.|
|Heal|[Ally](/srd/combat/attacks#term-ally-ability)|3 [MP](/srd/heroes/numbers#term-mp), 1 [AP](/srd/combat/round#term-ap)|The Paladin restores an amount of [HP](/srd/heroes/numbers#term-hp) to a single target equal to the Paladin's Magical Attack.|
