Tricksters exist to throw the [party](/srd/glossary#term-party) off guard.  They have high [Evasion](/srd/heroes/numbers#term-evasion) and can use [Trickster](/srd/heroes/archetypes/thief) [abilities](/srd/glossary#term-ability) to disrupt their opponents' usual tactics.

### New Ability

|Name|Type|Cost|Description|
|:-|:-|:-|:-|
|Taunt|[Ranged](/srd/combat/attacks#term-ranged)|1 [AP](/srd/combat/round#term-ap)|The Trickster uses a [sleight](/srd/combat/attacks#term-sleight) that inflicts the Taunted [status](/srd/combat/statuses) on a single target on a hit until the beginning of the Trickster's next turn, not counting held turns.  A Taunted [character](/srd/glossary#term-character) can only use single-target [spells](/srd/combat/attacks#term-spell), [strikes](/srd/combat/attacks#term-strike), and sleight [abilities](/srd/glossary#term-ability), and only on the character who inflicted it.  It can also use the [Move](/srd/combat/round#term-move) [action](/srd/glossary#term-action) to move into range of their target if needed.  Each character can only be Taunted by a single other character at a time.  If a Taunted character cannot affect the Trickster by any means, they will act as though they are not Taunted.|
