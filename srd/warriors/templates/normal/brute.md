Brutes are [warriors](/srd/warriors) with high defensive [statistics.](/srd/glossary#term-statistic)  They are best used as [front row](/srd/combat/starting#term-front-row) fighters that can protect their more fragile allies.

### Available Abilities

|Name|[Type](/srd/combat/attacks#term-ability-type)|Cost|Description|
|:-|:-|:-|:-|
|Taunt|[Ranged](/srd/combat/attacks#term-ranged)|1 [AP](/srd/combat/round#term-ap)|The Defender uses a [sleight](/srd/combat/attacks#term-sleight) that inflicts the Taunted [status](/srd/combat/statuses) on a single target on a hit until the beginning of the Defender's next turn, not counting held turns.  A Taunted [character](/srd/glossary#term-character) can only use single-target [spells](/srd/combat/attacks#term-spell), [strikes](/srd/combat/attacks#term-strike), and sleight [abilities](/srd/glossary#term-ability), and only on the character who inflicted it.  It can also use the [Move](/srd/combat/round#term-move) [action](/srd/glossary#term-action) to move into range of their target if needed.  Each character can only be Taunted by a single other character at a time.  If a Taunted character cannot affect the Defender by any means, they will act as though they are not Taunted.|
|Shield|[Self](/srd/combat/attacks#term-self-ability)|4 MP, 1 AP|The Defender inflicts the Shielded status on themselves.  While a character is Shielded, if they are hit by a strike the damage dealt to them is automatically reduced to 0 and the Shielded status effect is removed.|
