    Extra abilities for Elite warriors and Antagonists.

[Elite](/srd/warriors/creation#term-elite-warrior) [warriors](/srd/warriors) and [Antagonists](/srd/anima/antagonists) are meant to feel more dangerous than their [Normal](/srd/warriors/creation#term-normal-enemy) counterparts.  [GMs](/srd/warriors/templates/elite-abilities) are encouraged to give them custom abilities like the examples written below in order to achieve this effect.

|Name|Type|Cost|Description|
|:-|:-|:-|:-|
|Necrotize|[Ranged](/srd/combat/attacks#term-ranged)|1 [AP](/srd/heroes/numbers#term-ap), 3 [MP](/srd/heroes/numbers#term-mp)|The warrior uses a [sleight](/srd/combat/attacks#term-sleight) that, on a hit, inflicts the Necrotized [status,](/srd/combat/statuses#term-status) which means that all direct healing done to the target is instead taken as damage.|
|Puppeteer|Ranged|1 AP, 5 MP|The warrior uses a sleight that, on a hit, lets them force the target to take a 1 AP action of their choice.|
|Drain Life|[Melee](/srd/combat/attacks#term-melee)|2 AP, 3 MP|The warrior makes a [strike](/srd/combat/attacks#term-strike) that, on a hit, deals colorless damage and heals them for the same amount of damage dealt.|
|Damage Mana|Ranged|1 AP, 5 MP|The warrior uses a [spell](/srd/combat/attacks#term-spell) that, on a hit, deals colorless damage to the target's MP rather than their HP.|
|Push/Pull|[Multi](/srd/combat/attacks#term-multi)|2 AP, 5 MP|The warrior uses a sleight that, on a hit, causes every target in one of the two [rows](/srd/combat/starting#term-row) of the battlefield to move to the other.|
|Double Strike|Melee|1 AP|The warrior makes two [basic attacks](/srd/combat/round#term-basic-attack) against a single target.|
|Drain AP|Melee|1 AP|The warrior makes a strike that reduces the AP of the target by 1 on a hit.|
|Sting|Melee|1 AP, 3 MP|The warrior makes a basic attack that also inflicts a status on the target of some kind.|
|Bargain|[Special](/srd/combat/attacks#term-special)|1 AP, 10 MP|The warrior selects a target.  They offer to grant them a boon, such as a [charm,](/srd/combat/glamors#term-charm) right now in exchange for a cost that will be paid in 1d4 rounds in the warrior has not been [downed.](/srd/combat/injuries#term-downed)|
|Consume the Dead|Special|Passive|During the [Effect Phase,](/srd/combat/round#term-effect-phase) the warrior can inflict [True Death](/srd/anima/true-death) on a downed ally in exchange for a significant benefit, such as healing to full HP or MP.|
