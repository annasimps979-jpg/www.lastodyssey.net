[Warriors](/srd/warriors/templates/passives) live within their own context.  To express this, and give them additional tactical depth, <abbr title = "game masters">[GMs](/srd/glossary#term-gm)</abbr> can give them any passive ability granted by [equipment upgrades.](/srd/economy/equipment/upgrades)  A number of examples of appropriate passive abilities are listed below.

|Name|Description
|:-|:-|
|Elemental Immunity|The warrior is immune to a given type of [elemental damage.](/srd/combat/elements)|
|Elemental Absorption|When the warrior would take elemental damage, they instead regain either [HP,](/srd/heroes/numbers#term-hp) [MP,](/srd/heroes/numbers#term-mp) or [Shell](/srd/combat/shells) equal to the amount of damage they would have taken.|
|Status Immunity|The warrior is immune to a single extra [status.](/srd/combat/statuses#term-status)|
|Hollow Body|The warrior takes damage from [blessings,](/srd/heroes/roles/healer#term-blessing) but is immune to colorless damage.|
|Hex Immunity|The warrior is immune to two types of [hexes.](/srd/combat/glamors#term-hex)|
|Status Trigger|When the warrior receives a certain status, they also receive another.  For example, they start regenerating when [Poisoned,](/srd/combat/statuses#term-poisoned) or they become [Focused](/srd/heroes/archetypes/knight) when [Taunted.](/srd/heroes/roles/defender)|
|Revenge|When the warrior is downed, they use an extra ability against the [character](/srd/glossary#term-character) who downed them.|
|Tough Hide|The warrior must take at least 5 damage in order to receive any damage from any sources.  This can be increased to 10 or 15 depending on how powerful the warrior is.|
|Handler|There is another warrior on the battlefield that shares an AP pool with this one.|
|Reinforcements|Every Effect Phase, cast 1d4.  If the result is a 1, another of this warrior appears on the battlefield.|
